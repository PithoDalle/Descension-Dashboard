The Shadowy Search Continues

See if other townsfolk have clues about the shadowy figure.
Book from Sven's Farm (Provided)

Description

Here is your book back, <name>, and I bid you fortune with your search. If you ask others in town, perhaps they can offer you more clues.

And here is one more for you - although the author of this book is a stranger to Darkshire, the book itself was purchased here.

Progress

Sometimes I'll sell blank books to scholars and mages, or to anyone who wants to keep a journal.

I remember well everyone to whom I sold a book within the last few months...

Completion

Ah, yes. I sold this book a month ago to a shady person. He didn't tell me his name, but he was a nervous, jittery chap, always looking over his shoulder.

I hope that helps you...
