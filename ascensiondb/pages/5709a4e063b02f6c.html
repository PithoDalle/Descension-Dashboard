Knowledge of the Centaurs

Search the centaur camp for clues to the flower’s properties.

Description

This isn’t enough…it’s a start, yes. But this task will require more. For a start, I need to understand this flower’s properties. I’ve yet to find anyone in the Horde who knows of it, they don’t seem to grow very frequently. But there is another group who may know of natural things.

The centaurs have lived in Durotar for many years, not that we can just ask them what they know. Still, it’s possible they have some record of this flower. See what you can learn from their camp. I’ll be in Orgrimmar when you’re done.

Be safe. You are already a stronger and braver warrior than I, but with strength and bravery often come foolishness, as much as many orcs are loathe to admit it.

Progress

I can only hope they keep some form of record.

Completion

A crude drawing seems to depict the flower Sakari spoke of, next to a winged elf woman. Several more crude drawings next to it show the winged elf woman carrying various ores and gems. Perhaps whatever is being depicted knows more about the flower, and would value ores and gems.
