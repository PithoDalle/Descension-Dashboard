Cutting Teeth

Kill 8 Mottled Boars then return to Gornek at the Den.
  •  
Mottled Boar slain (8)

Description

The first order of business will be to put a little strength in your backbone. I could send you out to the Barrens to hunt kodo, but well, in all honesty, you're more useful to us alive than dead.

I believe you would find a good match with the mottled boars you'll find to the north of here.

Progress

I hope you haven't returned to try and convince me that you have completed your task, <name>? No, of course not. I would think better of you.

Completion

Hmmm, not bad, <name>. But don't let it get to your head...you'll fight tougher than boars in your career.

Nonetheless, you've proven yourself well, and your next trial will be against a considerably more dangerous opponent, so you'll need some extra protection.

Rewards

You will be able to choose one of these rewards:
Battleworn Leather Gloves Soft Wool Boots
You will also receive: 1 20 if completed at level 80
Adventurer's Cache Small Brown Pouch
Mark of Ascension

Gains

Upon completion of this quest you will gain:
  • 215 experience
  • 250 reputation with Orgrimmar
  • 250 reputation with Darkspear Trolls
