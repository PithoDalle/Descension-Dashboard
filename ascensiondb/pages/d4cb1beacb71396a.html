Unsealing The Crypts: A Spirit's Dream

Find the Crypt Keystone hidden within the realm of death.
  •  
Locate the Keystone in the Realm of Spirits
Provided item:
Vial of Ebonbloom

Description

Now that I have a means of using it properly, we'll need the keystone Aegwynn created to bind the seal to. She kept the keystone close to the crypts of course, the closer it is to the seal the better it can sustain it. But if it was that simple, it would've been found, no?

The keystone is phased out of the physical world. You can go right in front of the crypts and see nothing at all. Because it is used to bind the spirits of the dead within the crypts, the stone is instead found at the crypt's entrance within the Spirit World that overlaps Azeroth.

Of course, Aegwynn had her own way of retrieving it, but given I do not have access to such magics, you'll need to find a solution yourself.

Completion

I hate seeing more people doing this...if I could have just done something back then, if I had just stopped all of this...

But it's what they would have wanted, for you to find a way to settle this. And if the spirit healers favor you, maybe you really can do something. Maybe this can finally end...

Sorry, I'm talking too much, aren't I? I always do this. Like I said, I can't do much to actually effect the world outside the Emerald Dream, but I'll help you the best I can.
