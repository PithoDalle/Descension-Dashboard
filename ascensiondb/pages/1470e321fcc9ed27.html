Mission: Gateways Murketh and Shaadraz

Speak with Wing Commander Brack, bomb Gateway Murketh and Gateway Shaadraz and then return to Forward Commander To'arch at Reaver's Fall.
Seaforium PU-36 Explosive Nether Modulator (Provided)
  •  
Gateway Murketh Destroyed
  •  
Gateway Shaadraz Destroyed

Description

We've identified the two main transporters that the Burning Legion are using to bring in reinforcements: Gateway Murketh and Gateway Shaadraz.

If you look east up the Path of Glory, you'll see them on either side of the road. I want you to bomb them both into oblivion!

Here are the bombs you'll use, specially designed by a goblin in Area 52. Now, speak with Wing Commander Brack -- he'll put you on a wyvern.

Progress

If you missed during your first pass, take another...

Completion

That will set them back weeks! Fantastic work, <name>. The Legion never expected such heavy resistance.

Rewards

You will be able to choose one of these rewards:
Raging Spirit Harness Helm of Infinite Visions
Battle Scarred Leggings Protectorate Breastplate
You will also receive: 3 3 if completed at level 80
Mystic Orb Mark of Ascension

Gains

Upon completion of this quest you will gain:
  • 12,600 experience
  • 350 reputation with Thrallmar
This quest will be converted to Mission: The Murketh and Shaadraz Gateways if you transfer to Alliance.
