Becoming a Dark Ranger

Collect the Dark Ranger's Regalia.
Dark Ranger's Hood
Dark Ranger's Bow
Dark Ranger's Arrows
Pristine Elven Skull

Description

Items Needed:

Dark Ranger's Hood
Dark Ranger's Bow
Dark Ranger's Arrows
Pristine Elven Skull

Recover the stolen ensemble of a Dark Ranger.

The Dark Ranger's Hood can be obtained from hooded figures throughout the Plaguelands.
The Dark Ranger's Bow can be acquired by those who don the Scarlet uniform in Western Plaguelands.
The Dark Ranger's Arrows can be found from those who've become victim to the Dark Ranger within the Ruins of Andorhal in Western Plaguelands.
The Pristine Elven Skull can be found from High Elven rangers who reside within the Plaguelands.

Progress

You feel a rush of adrenaline and a sense of power after completing such an evil act. You revel in the chaos you've had created, feeling a thrill of excitement from your ability to cause destruction and harm. As you survey the aftermath of your actions, you felt a sense of satisfaction and pride in the fear and chaos you've wrought. Your once-principled morals had been cast aside, replaced by a hunger for power and a thirst for violence. The adrenaline-fueled high of committing evil acts was intoxicating, and you feel a twisted sense of joy in your newfound power.
