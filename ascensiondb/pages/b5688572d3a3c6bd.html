To Find a Cure

Find the strange flower.
Strange Flower

Description

Hmm. You mix the peacebloom and…no, that isn’t quite right.

The new recruit, right? Maybe you could help with something. Consider it part of your trials, maybe. Fighting was never my strength. I underwent these trials a few years back, a scorpid almost killed me. I only survived by making a salve with the local cacti and a rare flower.

Not that it did much good. Do you think I’d be cheered on for coming back a failure? I’m a weakling who failed and a coward who couldn’t finish her fight.

But I’m back here anyways, not because I plan to repeat that but because I found my own path in life, and I’m trying to find a cure to something that’s afflicted us for a while. So if you do better in your trials than I did, I’m looking for another one of those flowers I found. Not for a healing salve this time. Something else. In my trials I found it in the north-west, growing under a tree.

Progress

I can only hope another flower has grown since that day.

Completion

Growing here in this harsh landscape, in spite of everything against it.

Rather familiar, no?

When I applied that salve, I noticed something odd. For a few moments, the green tint in my skin faded away on my chest, where I’d applied it. Like it was healing not just the scorpid’s sting, but the Legion’s influence as well.

Even after all these years, the corruption of the fel blood the orcs drank long ago still remains. Green skin, but not just that. Our blood is forever tainted by demons, and it’s hard to say what influence that has. Subtle, perhaps, but it’s always there, within the orcs…and I’m going to find a cure. I might not have strength, or courage…and a lot of us would argue that means I lack honor as well. But I have a knowledge of herbs and alchemy. All I need is the help of someone willing to do what I cannot.

What do you say? Will you aid a weakling and a coward?
