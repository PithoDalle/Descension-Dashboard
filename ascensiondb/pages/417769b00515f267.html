Description

Deep down in the caverns below the Blackrock Mountain lies a place the Dark Iron Dwarves call the "Molten Core". A chasm filled with golems made of lava, fire elementals and other fiery creatures. They all serve the being known as Ragnaros the Firelord, one of the elemental lords residing in the elemental planes deep inside Azeroth.

Our spy reports tell us that they are preparing the final rituals to summon Ragnaros to our world. The summoning of an elemental Lord could cause great disasters and destruction all around Azeroth.

That must be prevented at any cost! Gather an army, travel to the Molten Core and slay anyone that stands in your way.
