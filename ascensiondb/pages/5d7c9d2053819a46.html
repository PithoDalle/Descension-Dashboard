Treasure Hunt #1

Find the first clue.

Description

Hidden behind the shadow of the Firelord;
Upon an unholy altar I lie dormant.

Completion

As you look into the Mysterious Orb you feel yourself enter a trance. As hard as you try you cannot stray your gaze from the orb. You notice letters form on its surface.

"Ah, so you've made it this far. Now the challenge really begins. Listen closely if you wish to continue upon your journey and seek the treasure which lies inevitably for you.

'Travel west to a land of bones,
Then west again where serpents roam.
Near secluded isle I do reside,
Anchored away beneath the tide.'

Best of luck, Champion."
