Shinies!

Collect metallic debris.
Metallic Debris (6)

Description

You recognize the drawing as a creature you have heard threatens Durotar, a harpy. Perhaps gems or ore will appease one enough to talk with you.

You search for shinies? Yes, yes, yes, come back with shinies, maybe not eat you!
