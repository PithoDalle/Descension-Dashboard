Description

Hero, remnants of magical artifacts litter Netherstorm, soaked in arcane energies. Crystals, runestones, magical texts - if gathered, these could be studied by our allies to unlock their power and reverse engineer them for good. I ask you venture there, battle its twisted denizens, and scavenge what magical artifacts you can find. Bring them to me so their secrets may empower our cause.
