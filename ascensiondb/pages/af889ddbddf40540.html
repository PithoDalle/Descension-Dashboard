Description

Lei'shen was once the ruler of a great empire, known for his mastery of thunder and lightning magic. Before his downfall, he created a powerful idol that was said to grant its wielder immense power and control over the elements. However, the idol was shattered into four relics and scattered across the land, to prevent its power from falling into the wrong hands.

Gather and reunite all four relics, and harness the power of the Thunder King.

These four relics are said to have been scattered throughout the ruins within the desert.
