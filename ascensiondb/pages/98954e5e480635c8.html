A Dangerous Sample

Collect demon blood from a strong demon.
Xaitoth's Blood

Description

You have my gratitude. The truth is, this flower is the only lead I have. I’ve gathered plenty of cacti, there’s enough around I didn’t need to venture much off the main road, but the property I observed must have been in the flower. It’s more than most anyone else has found, but I have limited resources, and we don’t even know where the flower originated.

What I need to understand is the properties of the demon blood. I’ve taken plenty of my own as samples, but I need something from the source. There’s demons in the cave at the north end of the valley, no? Find me the blood of one there, not any blood, the strongest you can find.

It will not compare to what the orcs drank of long ago, but perhaps it is at least a start.

Progress

You may have to search the cave for one strong enough for my purposes. Don't do anything reckless.

Completion

I know the Horde values strength and courage, but as someone without either, I do not like asking you to face against a demon of such power for my sake. Still, I thank you. It is my hope now that this can lead us to a cure. Not right now, maybe, but I am young yet.

Give me a moment, I’ll likely have more requests.

Rewards

You will receive: 90 if completed at level 80
Adventurer's Cache Mark of Ascension

Gains

Upon completion of this quest you will gain:
  • 170 experience
  • 250 reputation with Orgrimmar
  • 250 reputation with Darkspear Trolls
