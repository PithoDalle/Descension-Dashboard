Wonderful Wood: Seasoned Wood Log

Collect 20 Seasoned Wood Log
Seasoned Wood Log (20)

Description

Attention, brave adventurers of Azeroth! We are facing a dire timber crisis that threatens the very core of our society. Our once-thriving realm is paralyzed by a shortage of wood planks, leaving buildings unfinished, repairs neglected, and our dreams of progress shattered. We urgently require the assistance of a hero to delve into the mystical forests, navigate treacherous paths, and gather precious timber from hidden groves. Your valorous efforts will not go unrewarded, as a nifty prize awaits those who can help us overcome this catastrophe. Join us in our fight to restore abundance and prosperity to Azeroth. Report to the quest board now and be hailed as the savior of our realm! Together, we shall overcome this timber crisis and build a brighter future for all.

Rewards

You will receive:
Callboard Cache Mystic Rune
Mystic Orb Mark of Ascension
