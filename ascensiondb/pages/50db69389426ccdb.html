Aelric's Curse

Collect the items Aelric needs to break his bondage.
Moondust Tears
Shard of Fel Ruin
Seal of the Forgotten
Sorrowbrand Relic

Description

Another soul drawn to the blade... another pawn of my sorrow.

Whisper... my soul...

I forged her in defiance of the Legion... yet she was torn from me by betrayal and death.

Ever since, my spirit has wandered, reaching across the veil, clinging to the blade that was once part of me."

The War of the Ancients shattered more than stone and flesh. It shattered oaths. It shattered faith.

Whisper is cursed not by evil, but by longing... my longing.

You, mortal... you have heard the cries, have you not? The screams through the blade?

They were mine. They were the others, too... countless souls drawn into Whisper's shadow.

<Aelric lowers his head>

I beg you. Sever the chains.

Collect the relics necessary to perform the cleansing that will remove my bondage from this weapon. I require the following items:

Moondust Tears, said to be dropped by Anguished Highborne in Winterspring, the Seal of the Forgotten, said to be dropped by Eldreth Sorcerers in Dire Maul, and a Shard of Fel Ruin, said to be dropped by Doomguard Commanders in the Blasted Lands.

I'll also require the Sorrowbrand Relic - an ancient artifact used in old Highborne rituals... I believe your little friend back in the swamp may have found it.

Only then will Whisper be free... and so too shall I be.

Progress

Could it be? Someone who was capable of collecting all of the necessary items...

Completion

You have done well. I am now unshackled from my bondage with Whisper... and in return?

I give it to you. May it serve you as it once did me, without the torment that it has brought upon the world.

Rewards

You will receive: 5
Whisper Mark of Ascension
