Galgar's Cactus Apple Surprise

Bring Galgar 6 Cactus Apples. You remember him saying that they could be found near cactuses.
Cactus Apple (6)

Description

It sure gets hot out here in the Valley of Trials.

<Galgar wipes his brow.>

If only I had some cactus apples, I could make my famous cactus apple surprise! Nothing cools you off faster than a piece of that delicious treat.

I'll tell you what, <name>. If you bring me 6 cactus apples, I'll make you a few portions of cactus apple surprise to take with you on your adventures. If you're interested, you can find cactus apples growing near the cactus plants around here.

Progress

Have you finished collecting cactus apples?

Completion

Wonderful, <name>! As promised, here is your cactus apple surprise plus a little something extra.

Rewards

You will receive: 50 (or 2 40 if completed at level 80)
Adventurer's Cache Cactus Apple Surprise
Mark of Ascension

Gains

Upon completion of this quest you will gain:
  • 380 experience
  • 500 reputation with Orgrimmar
  • 500 reputation with Darkspear Trolls
