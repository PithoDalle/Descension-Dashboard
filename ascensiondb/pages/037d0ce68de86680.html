Drop of Whispering Blood

Feed Blood of the Heroes to the corpse.
Blood of Heroes (5)

Description

The corpse before you is entirely drained of blood. The closer you get to the corpse the stronger the desire to open your veins and give the gift of your life's blood to corpse. You sense that once you begin that ritual there would be no turning back until you were as dry as the corpse before you.

Perhaps there is another way to feed the corpse?

Progress

You feed the Blood of Heroes to corpse before you. The blood is drawn into the corpse's fangs with magnetic force.

The body seems to gain life for the briefest moment before a single drop of blood pools from the spigot attached to its desiccated heart.

Completion
