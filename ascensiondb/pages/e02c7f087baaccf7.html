Whispers from the Dying

Travel to Blighthollow and locate the entrance.

Description

You there! A moment.

A guard stumbled into the Sepulcher not long ago bearing horrific wounds unlike any I have seen before. His flesh rots unnaturally, and the stench of decay clings to him still.

Before he lost consciousness, he spoke of Blighthollow...

I know of Blighthollow. Most of us do, though few speak of it. A research facility, deep in the crypts to the south. We haven't heard from the facility in some time, and now I fear something has gone terribly wrong.

Someone needs to find out what happened. The Undercity will want answers, and right now we don't have enough guards to spare.

Go to Blighthollow. Find out what is going on. And if there is anyone left in there, bring them out.

Progress

Have you found Blighthollow? If anyone remains alive within those crypts, the Forsaken must know.

Completion

You... you're not one of them, are you? Then perhaps there is still hope for me.

I was a patrol guard here. I saw things that were never meant to be seen... hidden experiments, forbidden movements, guards turning blind eyes to unspeakable acts.

I did what any loyal soldier would do and reported everything to my commander.

Next dusk I was thrown into one of these cells. No trial. No explanation. Nothing but "Treason against the Dark Lady".

I have rotted here long enough to hear horrors no man should endure. Please... free me.
