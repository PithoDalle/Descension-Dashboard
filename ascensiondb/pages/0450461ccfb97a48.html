Proof of Demise

Bring 10 Scarlet Insignia Rings to Deathguard Burgess in Brill.
Scarlet Insignia Ring (10)

Description

Executor Zygand tells me you've been out killing Scarlet Crusaders. Tirisfal Glades is crawling with them.

Varimathras, lieutenant to The Dark Lady, is seeing to it personally that the scum of Azeroth, the Human race, gets wiped into oblivion. Here in Tirisfal Glades we face a particularly annoying breed of humans: The Scarlet Crusade.

Venture forth and wage an offensive on the Scarlet Crusade, <class>. Bring me back 10 Scarlet Insignia Rings as proof of your loyalty to Varimathras and The Dark Lady.

Progress

Have you collected 10 Scarlet Insignia Rings yet, <class>?

Completion

Varimathras would be proud. You serve your leaders well, <class>.

Rewards

You will be able to choose one of these rewards:
Zombie Skin Bracers Clasped Belt
Netted Gloves
You will also receive: 1 75 (or 3 90 if completed at level 80)
Adventurer's Cache

Gains

Upon completion of this quest you will gain:
  • 630 experience
  • 250 reputation with Undercity
