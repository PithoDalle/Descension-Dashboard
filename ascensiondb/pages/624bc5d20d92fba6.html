Path to Ascension: Journey to the Capital

Enter the Deeprun Tram. Wait on the platform for the Tram to arrive, then speak to Bromey Sockets when you arrive at the Stormwind station.

Description

The time has come to journy to the capital <race>! Stormwind lies far across the mountains. The passes are impossible to travel by foot, but the marvels of gnomish engineering have created the Deeprun Tram.

Enter the Deeprun Tram directly behind me.

Wait for the tram to arrive and then enter the cart. Keep your hands, feet and swords inside at all times. You do NOT want to find yourself walking because you peered a little too far over the edge.

When you arrive at the Stormwind station, depart the tram and speak to Bromey Sockets for further instruction.

Completion

The Deeprun Tram runs day and night. You can always return here to quickly travel between Stormwind and Ironforge. Exit the Deeprun Tram to the north of me. Travel to the Trade District and present yourself to Justine Demalier.
