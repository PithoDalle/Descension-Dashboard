Purge of the Dragonkiller's Bloodline

Take the Heavy Sack of Heads to Betrayer Grondstok
Sack of Heads (Provided)

Description

The dreaded Sons of Gruul have been decapitated at last. Their severed heads contained in this sack are evidence of your heroic deeds on behalf of the people of Outland.

Gruul bred his progeny to be as cunning as they were brutal, but their centuries of cruel dominion have been brought to an end. The fearful shadows cast across the land by their cyclopean forms will stretch no longer.

Take the sack of severed heads to Betrayer Grondstok. He will know what cleansing rituals must be done to purification against any vestiges of demonic corruption the Sons carried. The gratitude of all free people goes with you.
