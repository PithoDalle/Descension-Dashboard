Blood Samples

Bring your gathered blood samples to Sakari so that we may study the properties of pit lord vitae and better understand the true power and danger of fel blood.

Description

Research into the properties of the fel blood is progressing slowly as expected. Some progress has been made regarding how it relates to the standard fel corruption other orcs experience. Several substances may potentially aid in understanding the properties of fel blood. - The Blood of Mannoroth, most seems to have been consumed by the orcs already, and no method of reaching the demon Mannoroth has proven feasible. - Pit Lord's Blood, potentially from the demon Magtheridon imprisoned in this area, for understanding properties specific to Mannoroth's blood. Besides these, there seem to be several artificial substances created by the Fel Horde. Perhaps some of these have managed to replicate properties of demon blood, provided they are not - Abyssal Elxir, seems to be a potion created to empower the orcs. - Blood of the Legions, besides the Fel Orc's vague naming, may provide different properties to study? - Demonic Elixir, possibly similar to Abyssal Elixir, Fel Orcs seem to describe it as a separate substance though. Unfortunately I lack the means of fighting past the Fel Orcs to obtain any of these, and have not been able to procure additional support in my research besides these prisoners. This platform on Hellfire Citadel seems to remain isolated at least and it is my hope that it remains secure for the foreseeable future.

Completion

You found the samples? I'd abandoned that manifest after it was exposed to the fel blood, I didn't expect anyone to actually go looking for these... Well, thank you. I've spent most of my life trying to understand the properties of fel corruption, and more importantly, how to fully remove it from my people. I may have found a way once...well, it's not one I can try again for a while yet. I came here hoping that the more intense corruption of the fel orcs here might help me understand it better. With these, maybe I can actually make some progress.
