Steelsnap

Bring Steelsnap's Rib to Melor Stonehoof in Thunder Bluff.
Steelsnap's Rib

Description

At times we hunt for food. At times we hunt for honor. And at times we hunt to earn the Earthmother's teachings.

But the hunt I now set upon you is none of these things. It is to slay a creature outside the Earthmother's blessing.

The hyena Steelsnap roams the Thousand Needles, south of the Barrens. He is a tyrant, attracting other hyenas to him and spreading fear and bloodshed among more peaceful beasts.

Find Steelsnap, <name>. Find him, and defeat him.

Progress

Have you defeated the foul Steelsnap, <name>?

Completion

In defeating the creature Steelsnap, you have cleansed the Thousand Needles of a horrible taint.

And that, <name>, is your lesson this day.

Rewards

You will receive: 15 if completed at level 80
Adventurer's Cache Mark of Ascension

Gains

Upon completion of this quest you will gain:
  • 2,450 experience
  • 250 reputation with Thunder Bluff
