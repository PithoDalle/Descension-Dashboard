Night Web's Hollow

Executor Arren wants you to kill 8 Young Night Web Spiders and 5 Night Web Spiders.
  •  
Young Night Web Spider slain (8)
  •  
Night Web Spider slain (5)

Description

One of our greatest struggles is obtaining the natural resources we need to survive. Gold was scarce even in the height of the Alliance's power.

There is a gold mine to the northwest that has been overrun with spiders. We need gold from the mine, but we can't very well get it while the spiders are crawling around in there. I've not much manpower to commit to the task, so we'll just have to do it little by little.

Get up there and see what you can do for us, <name>.

Progress

Be wary of the spiders' venom, <name>. If you feel a sharp burning, you may want to have it looked at.

Completion

Hmm, well, it's a start. It'll take a few weeks or months to fully clean out the infestation. After that, we'll have to get down there with some torches to burn away the webbing.

You've done your duty well, <name>, I'm sure I can find something else for you to do.
