The Denholms' Rest

Help Father Dayton put the spirits of the Denholm family to rest at the Dead Field.
  •  
Alkuin Denholm Put to Rest
  •  
Marlis Denholm Put to Rest
Provided item:
Father Dayton's Talisman

Description

The Denholm family used to come here every week to bring flowers for those of the dead who had no one else to remember them. They kind souls.

I hear now their farmstead is known as the Dead Field. It must have been a horrible fate. I have not felt their souls pass on, yet they are not amongst the dead who continue to walk here.

It is beyond my means to do anything for them, bound as my soul is to my grave, but there is a small talisman atop my monument that still carries some of my power. Could I trouble you to take it to the Denholm Farmstead...the Dead Field...and set their spirits free, if they truly remain there?

Progress

The Denholm family cared for their home as much as I cared for these graves, so I am quite sure their spirits would not have left.

Completion

They are finally at peace?

I am sorry you had to meet them in such a state, living the fear and anger of their dying moments. I can promise you that they would have never tried to bring harm otherwise.

I know all too well the feeling of a duty that death cannot take you from. Their home has been gone for a long time, and remaining there would only bring them more pain. You can put that talisman back on my grave, I still have a purpose in remaining here. When that is no longer true, perhaps then I shall rest.
