Treasure Hunt #1

Find the third clue.

Description

The words written on the orb seem etched into your memory.

'I desire so furiously to be found,
But my cries fall quiet underground.
Above me faintly I hear the thunder roar,
Drowned out by snarls of demon I abhor.'

You feel confident that this will lead you to the third clue.

Completion

You notice the orb glowing more fervently than ever this time as it takes ahold of you once more.

"Champion, oh my champion! You have proven you are in fact worthy of my gift! I have but one final test, and that is to free me so that I may give you the power you so long for and deserve. Now listen closely one final time as the devil is in the details.

'Exiled to a land where the dead roam free,
There wanders a man who holds the key.
Amongst the ruins he does reside,
Within a shelter he cowers and hides.
A path of righteousness he will implore you to take,
But be wary of the deceit he makes.
His blood must flow and the key you must take,
For then from my prison will I espcape.
Once the deed is done and you hold what I need,
Travel to where winter never recedes.
To a place that's hidden and sacred to few,
And unlock the secrets you wish you knew.'

I have the utmost faith in you my noble champion. Make haste and do not falter in this final task!"
