Collection of Heads

The betrayer has set out on a gruesome task. You know not what grim ritual they are meant for, but the betrayer promises great reward for completing this gory harvest.
Head of Durn the Hungerer
Head of Slaag

Description

Gruul control ogres, in cave, too strong to fight without army. Gruul's sons second strongest, but only second. Still stronger than Grondstok, but Grondstok know ways to take their strength, make it his. No, make it ours. Grondstok not want war with Draenei, not want serve Gruul. With heads of Gruul sons, we start rebellion.

Sons too strong, so Grondstok waits, but you get help, slay sons, and Grondstok give you strength of gronn.

Rewards

You will receive: 87 if completed at level 80

Gains

Upon completion of this quest you will gain:
  • 2,380 experience
