An Enhanced Experience

Collect 10 Strange Stinky Trimmings.
Strange Stinky Trimmings (10)

Description

Are you looking for something to really enhance your experience?

I have just the thing that may work for you, provided you will work for me. I'm way too chill right now to be running around gathering herbs. If you find 10 Strange Stinky Herbs and throughout the Plaguelands and bring their trimmings back to me I will pass on some most desirable skill cards to you.

Rewards

You will be able to choose one of these rewards:
Skill Card - Poisons Skill Card - Create Firestone
Skill Card - Flametongue Weapon Skill Card - Frostbrand Weapon
Skill Card - Windfury Weapon Skill Card - Earthliving Weapon
You will also receive:
Mark of Ascension
