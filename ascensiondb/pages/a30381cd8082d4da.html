Clambake Calamity

Collect Seaweed and Clam Meat in Azshara
Clam Meat (8)
Naga Flesh (15)
Seaweed (8)

Description

My friends and I are hosting a celebration at Booty Bay, a rare event where members of both the Alliance and Horde can come together, free of the ties of their factions, at least for a time. Everyone is counting on my famous, well, somewhat famous clam bake. I had planned to start making the dish today, but I discovered all my ingredients were stolen by pirates.

I use a specific variety of seaweed and clams found only in Azshara, but the celebration is tomorrow, and I don't have time to gather more. I need someone to gather clam meat and seaweed from Azshara, or this whole event will be a calamity.

Rewards

You will receive:
Callboard Cache Mystic Rune
Mystic Orb Mark of Ascension
