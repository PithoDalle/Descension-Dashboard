Treasure Hunt #1

Find the key and save the prisoner.
Darkrune Keystone

Description

The words written on the orb seem etched into your memory.

'Exiled to a land where the dead roam free,
There wanders a man who holds the key.
Amongst the ruins he does reside,
Within a shelter he cowers and hides.
A path of righteousness he will implore you to take,
But be wary of the deceit he makes.
His blood must flow and the key you must take,
For then from my prison will I escape.
Once the deed is done and you hold what I need,
Travel to where winter never recedes.
To a place that's hidden and sacred to few,
And unlock the secrets you wish you knew.'

You are filled with determination.

Progress

My Champion, do you have the key?

Completion

As you approach what you presume to be the final prison of the being trapped within you experience a new sensation. Instead of words whisping across the orb you see now a pair dark eyes stairing at you and you hear a voice enter your mind, though you are not sure from where it originates.

"Yes, yes my champion. You have done it! I knew from the moment I made contact with you that you were worthy of my gift. Now, place the rune upon the orb so that I can be free and share with you my limitless power!"

Rewards

You will receive:
Mark of Ascension
