Sven's Camp

Bring the Book from Sven's Farm to Sven.
Book from Sven's Farm (Provided)

Description

Digging through the mound, you find a small, dirt-stained book.

The book has no title, but...it has information Sven would like to know.

Progress

You found what?? Please, let me see it...

Completion

<Sven reads the book's pages, then slams it shut.>

That shadowy figure I saw was a witness to my family's death! There are questions I'd like to ask that man...if only I knew who he was!

Rewards

You will receive: 6 if completed at level 80

Gains

Upon completion of this quest you will gain:
  • 1,000 experience
  • 75 reputation with Stormwind
