Death Calls

Kill Troggs and loot their bones, flesh, and skull.

Description

In order to summon the Undead monstrosity, I must bring the following ingredients to the ritual circle.

- Bones
- Fresh Flesh
- Skull

The nearby Troggs have just what is needed.

Gains

Upon completion of this quest you will gain:
  • 25 experience
